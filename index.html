04 / The work

A planner that became an artist book.

The piece is a 2012 Daily Planner measuring 13.5 × 17.5 cm, with more than 350 pages, almost all of them used or intervened in some way.

Throughout the book, you’ll find drawings made with different techniques, collage, mixed media, ink, acrylic, pencil, stains, notes, textures, recovered pages, and finished compositions that appeared gradually over the years.

Nothing has been separated from the original object. The drawings, notes, marks, accidents and traces of everyday life remain together, preserving the book as one continuous record of time.
