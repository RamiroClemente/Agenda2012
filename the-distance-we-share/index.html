04 / The work

What remained when we were apart.

The Distance We Share was made in 2020, during countless hours of isolation through the pandemic.

Its 58 pages became a place to spend time when time itself seemed to have changed. Figures appear contained within the limits of the page, sometimes drawn and redrawn in layers, surrounded by distance, silence and the need for company.

India ink, collage, watercolor, soft pastel and transfers gradually accumulated on the paper. There was no intention to document the pandemic. I was simply drawing through it.

Looking at the book now, I see something I couldn't fully see then: a book made from time and resilience, but also from the memory of the people who remained part of my life while we were apart.

It is a unique artist's book and a very personal record of that period.
